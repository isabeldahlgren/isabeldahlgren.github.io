Everyone abides by a set of beliefs, whether they're religious or not. It's a matter of integrity. In this blog post, I'll list -- or confess -- some of mine.

Please don't take these as injunctions -- I'm addressing myself, not you. It's just that the text reads better with 'you' in place of 'I' everywhere.

- **Averages.** You are the time-weighted average of the people you spend your time with. Reading a book or listening to a podcast counts as spending time with the author or podcast host.
- **Beware bad averages.** Make friends with different kinds of people, read widely.
- **Corruption.** Money, prestige and fame corrupt. In particular, having a large number of social media followers corrupts.
- **A good measure of life satisfaction.** Just the number of good human interactions per day. Try maximise this number, both for yourself and for others. 
- **Randomness rules.** Group dynamics, network effects and the flukes of powerful people govern the world[^le]. In particular, the world is less predictable than you think.
[^le]: 'L'essentiel est invisible pour les yeux.'
- **You are a citizen.** This entails social duties well beyond having to vote. You ought to try solve some of our most pressing problems, to the best of your ability.
- **Everything is politics.** Everything acts on society. Companies are political organisations insofar as they act on society and their employees.
- **Communicate!** Overcommunicating is exceptionally rare. Relatedly, good things happen when goodwilled people have in-person discussions.
- **Concentrate!** Humans weren't meant to live alone. Families weren't meant to live scattered across different continents.
- **Kindness.** Kindness is underappreciated. If kindness is your distinguishing character trait, you're a laudable person.
- **Thinking well.** Poor writing leads to poor thinking.
- **The strongest motivations.** People just want to make their living and have quality time with their family. First things first. Oftentimes, the reason people are rude is because they cannot make their living or have quality time with their family.
- **Humans are craftsmen.** Self-realisation is just about honing a craft. We like applying ourselves. Self-realisation is a form of sadism, if you will[^ai].

[^ai]: Note: this means I'm very unexcited by the idea of an AI utopia where AI can give me custom paintings, poems or videos.

Many of my views can be derived from these beliefs -- those who know me well might recognise some of them. Comments and suggestions are much welcome. I'll offer a bounty to whoever makes me change my mind about any of the above.
